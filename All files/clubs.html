Explore Nous clubs

JCI

JCI Tunisia is part of Junior Chamber International, a global network of young active citizens committed to creating positive change. Through leadership development, community projects, and impactful events, JCI Tunisia empowers young people across the country to grow personally and professionally while making a difference in their communities..

TUNIVISION

Tunivisions Foundation is a Tunisian non-profit founded in 2016, focused on empowering youth through leadership development and civic engagement. It connects over 300 high school clubs, 188 university clubs, and 10,000+ alumni, offering hands-on experiences in cultural, artistic, and civic activities to foster proactive leadership.

4c

4C Tunisia (Centre de Carrière et de Certification des Compétences) is a university-based center that supports students and graduates in developing their professional skills, building their careers, and improving their employability. Through workshops, coaching, internships, and certifications, 4C acts as a bridge between academia and the job market..

AIESEC

AIESEC Tunisia is the national branch of the world’s largest youth-led organization. It empowers young people through leadership roles, international internships, and volunteer experiences that foster personal growth and global impact.

À propos de nous

Présentation de l’ISGB L’Institut Supérieur de Gestion de Bizerte (ISGB) est un établissement académique reconnu pour son excellence dans l’enseignement des sciences de gestion et de management
Notre mission Notre objectif est de former des cadres qualifiés et compétitifs, prêts à relever les défis du monde professionnel grâce à des programmes adaptés aux besoins du marché.
Pourquoi choisir l'ISGB ?

Avec un corps professoral expérimenté et un environnement dynamique, l’ISGB offre aux étudiants les outils nécessaires pour réussir dans les domaines du management, de la finance et du commerce.
